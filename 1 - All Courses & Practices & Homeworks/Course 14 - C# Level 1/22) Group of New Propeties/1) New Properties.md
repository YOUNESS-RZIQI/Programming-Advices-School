### Check Box Properties:



1\) PasswordChar: you can choose how to hide the chars



2\) Read Only: just showing the content of the TextBox



3\) Max Length: Maximum chars to hold in the TextBox



4\) Right To Left or Left To Right (default) and Inherit it does get the form cheap, because it is the default (Parent).



5\) Multy Line: can have newline "\\n"



6\) Word Wrap: it do a auto "\\n"



7\) Locked: you can't move the controls.



8\) Fucus: it get the curser on the text place.
